How the score works, and the weights

Score = weighted Value + weighted Feasibility − weighted Risk − weighted Effort, rescaled to 0–100. Each input is 1–5. Higher feasibility means the data, tools and people are closer to ready. The score sets priority order when projects compete for the same team capacity on the roadmap.

Readiness & guardrails

Score where the District is today, 0–100. Foundations raise these scores as they finish, which is what opens the gates for higher-risk work. Be candid: a low score delays work. It doesn't block a plan.

Readiness today

Planning assumptions

Capacity counts small projects (effort 1–2) as half a slot and large ones (effort 4–5) as one and a half. Foundations run as an enterprise program and don't use slots.

Gate rules

Minimum readiness score needed in the quarter a use case starts. The strict stance adds 10 points to sensitive and OT gates.

Non-negotiables for this window

  • No AI writes to SCADA, PLCs or control systems. Plant and collections models are advisory: an operator decides. Revisit after supervised trials and a cybersecurity review.
  • A human signs every regulatory submission. DMRs, annual reports and SSO reports are certified by staff, with every number traceable to LIMS or SCADA.
  • No AI in hiring or discipline decisions. Drafting help for HR documents is fine. Screening or ranking people is not.
  • Disclose AI on public-facing channels. Website assistants are labeled, and emergencies like sewer backups go straight to a person.
  • Treat prompts and outputs as potential public records. Use the District's sanctioned tools only, with retention set by policy.
  • No broad M365 Copilot until the cleanup is done. Copilot can reach only sites that have passed the Varonis review.
  • Infrastructure maps and control-system documents stay Restricted. They never go into consumer AI tools.

Reading the budget

  • Ranges are wide on purpose. The low end assumes existing licenses and light integration. The high end assumes new platforms and consultant help.
  • Watch the run-rate. Subscriptions keep billing after 2028. Plan year three before approving year one.
  • Grants can offset foundations. Cybersecurity and resilience grant programs may cover IT/OT and data work. Check current federal and state rounds.
  • To change a use case's numbers, open its row on the Portfolio tab and record the source.
